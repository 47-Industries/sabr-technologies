Privacy Policy

DRAFT of 2026-08-26  ·  Effective date: [not yet in effect]  ·  Contact: support@sabrtechnologies.com

This policy explains what Sabr Technologies (“Sabr”, “we”) collects when you use sabrtechnologies.com, start a trial, buy a synthetic intelligence (“SI”), or run one — and what we do with it. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to sell, install, and license the SI. Your SI’s mind lives on your machine, not on ours. We do not sell your data. Ever.

1.What we collect

Trial signup

  • Your name, email address, and the name you give your SI, from the trial form.
  • Where the trial variant verifies a card, the card itself is collected and stored by Stripe, not by us. Stripe gives us a non-reversible card fingerprint, which we keep solely to enforce one trial per card.

Purchases

  • Your email address, plan, and payment status, via Stripe checkout. We never see or store your full card number — payment details go directly to Stripe under Stripe’s own privacy policy.
  • The license token we mint for your install, tied to your purchase.

Install telemetry

  • While the installer runs, it mirrors its own progress log to us so we can help when an install goes sideways: your platform (operating system), installer version, the step-by-step progress lines the installer shows you on screen, the SI’s name, and the IP address the upload came from.
  • This is append-only text in one direction. It gives us no remote access, no shell, and no agent on your machine, and the install works the same if the upload fails.

License heartbeat

  • Once installed, the SI checks in with our license service about once a day. The check-in carries the license token (and, like any web request, exposes an IP address). Its only job is to answer one question: is this license still active?

Support and email

  • If you email support@sabrtechnologies.com (or the founder directly), we keep the correspondence so we can help you.

2.What we deliberately do not collect

Your SI’s mind is not our data. The SI’s memories, your conversations with it, your files, and everything it does on your machine live on your machine. In the standard (local) configuration, none of that is sent to Sabr — the heartbeat carries a license token, not content.
  • We do not receive your conversations or your SI’s memory state in the standard configuration.
  • We run no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics scripts on the site.
  • Where your SI’s responses are powered by your own account with a model provider (for example, your Claude subscription or API key with Anthropic), the content of those exchanges flows between your machine and that provider under that provider’s privacy policy — it does not pass through Sabr.
  • If Sabr hosts a brain for you (a hosted/managed configuration you explicitly chose), the state of that hosted brain necessarily lives on Sabr’s server; we treat it as your confidential data and access it only to operate and support your SI.

4.What we use it for

  • Delivering what you bought: minting tokens, sending install emails, provisioning, and licensing.
  • Helping you when something breaks — install logs exist so support does not start with “can you describe the error?”
  • Preventing abuse: one trial per person/card, and heartbeats that catch revoked licenses.
  • Telling you things about your own purchase or trial. We may also email you about the product; every non-transactional email has an unsubscribe that works.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not rent it, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

5.Retention and deletion

  • We keep purchase and license records for as long as your license is active, plus what tax and accounting law requires.
  • Install logs are operational debris; we keep them only as long as they are useful for support.
  • Deletion on request: email support@sabrtechnologies.com from the address on your account and ask. We will delete the personal data we hold about you, except the minimum we must keep by law (for example, records of a payment). We will confirm when it is done.
  • Data on your machine is yours to delete at any time — uninstalling the SI removes the software; the brain state directory is yours to keep or destroy.

6.Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it (above), or object to marketing. Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA/CPRA) you may have additional statutory rights; we honor requests from anywhere the same way. Write to support@sabrtechnologies.com. 7.Security

License tokens are random and fail closed; payment data never touches our servers; install-log uploads are one-directional and size-capped; admin access to logs is gated. No system is perfectly secure, but the architecture is built so the most sensitive thing — your SI’s mind — is never in our hands to lose.

8.Children

The site and the SI are not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age your jurisdiction sets), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9.Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will post the new version here with a new effective date and note the change. Continued use after that date means the new version applies.
